# Neon Hub art

The portraits, vehicle art and top-down tokens in this folder (`mooks/`, `rides/`, `tokens/`) were generated for Neon Hub with ElevenLabs'
image tools. They are fan art of the Cyberpunk 2077 setting (CD PROJEKT RED) and Cyberpunk RED (R. Talsorian
Games), made for free, non-commercial use with Neon Hub and the Cyberpunk RED - CORE system for Foundry VTT.
Neither CD PROJEKT RED nor R. Talsorian Games approved or endorsed them.

Use them freely in your own games. Don't sell them, and don't use them to suggest an official product.

The mooks' stat blocks are from *Corgo's 77 Collection: Mooks* by Corgopolis (patreon.com/Corgopolis), unofficial
content under R. Talsorian Games' Homebrew Content Policy; their descriptions are Neon Hub's own wording.
The REO Meatwagon and Combat Cab mooks (Sasquatch among them) are Neon Hub's own stat blocks.
